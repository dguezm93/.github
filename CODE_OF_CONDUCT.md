# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and explain feedback clearly.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's personal information without consent.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post sexualized content.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation to protect contributors. Report a concern through GitHub's reporting tools. Avoid posting private details in public discussions.
